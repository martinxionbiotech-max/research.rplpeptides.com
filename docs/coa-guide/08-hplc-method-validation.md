# HPLC Method Validation Protocol for Research Peptide Quality Control

!!! info "Executive Summary"
    Method validation ensures an HPLC analytical procedure consistently yields accurate, precise, and specific purity measurements for research peptides.

## Standard Validation Protocol Steps

1. **Solution Stability Testing**: Verify sample stability in autosampler at $4^\\circ\\text{C}$ over $24\\text{ h}$ and $48\\text{ h}$.
2. **Spike Recovery (Accuracy)**: Evaluate recovery across $80\\%$, $100\\%$, and $120\\%$ target concentrations (acceptance: $98.0\% - 102.0\%$).
3. **Linearity Range**: Assess detector response across $50\\%$ to $150\\%$ of nominal test concentration.

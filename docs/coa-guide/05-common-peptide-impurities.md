# Common Synthetic Peptide Impurities: Types, Causes, and Detection

Synthetic peptide impurities arise from incomplete couplings, side reactions during cleavage, or environmental degradation.

## Main Impurity Categories
1. **Deletion Peptides $(N-1)$**: Formed by coupling failure at a specific amino acid cycle.
2. **Oxidized Peptides**: Oxidation of Methionine or Tryptophan residues.
3. **Diastereomers**: Racemization during activation steps.

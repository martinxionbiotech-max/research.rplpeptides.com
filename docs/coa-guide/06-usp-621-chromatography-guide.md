# USP <621> Chromatography Guide: Allowable Method Adjustments

!!! info "Executive Summary"
    USP <621> specifies acceptable tolerances for adjusting HPLC parameters without re-validating the analytical method.

## Summary of Allowable Adjustments

- **Column Length ($L$) / Particle Size ($d_p$)**: $L/d_p$ ratio may be adjusted within $-25\\%$ to $+50\\%$.
- **Flow Rate**: May be adjusted by $\\pm 50\\%$.
- **Column Temperature**: May be adjusted by $\\pm 10^\\circ\\text{C}$ (up to a maximum of $60^\\circ\\text{C}$).
- **pH of Mobile Phase**: May be adjusted by $\\pm 0.2\\text{ pH units}$.
- **Injection Volume**: May be reduced as long as detection precision and $S/N$ criteria are satisfied.

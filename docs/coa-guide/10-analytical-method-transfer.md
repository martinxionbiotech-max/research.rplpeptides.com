# Analytical Method Transfer: Ensuring Inter-Laboratory Reproducibility

!!! info "Executive Summary"
    Method transfer verifies that a receiving laboratory can reproduce analytical purity results within pre-defined acceptance limits.

## Key Considerations in Transfer Protocols

1. **Dwell Volume ($V_D$) Matching**: Differences in system gradient delay volume ($V_D$) cause retention shifts in gradient elution.
2. **Column Lot-to-Lot Variability**: Stationary phase bonding density differences across C18 silica batches.
3. **Acceptance Criteria**: Mean purity difference between sending and receiving labs must be $\\le 0.5\\%$.

# Oxidized Peptide Impurities: Methionine, Tryptophan, and Cysteine

!!! info "Executive Summary"
    Oxidation of Methionine to Methionine Sulfoxide increases molecule polarity, shifting retention time earlier on reversed-phase HPLC columns.

## Characteristic Mass Shift
- **Methionine Sulfoxide**: $+16\\text{ Da}$
- **Methionine Sulfone**: $+32\\text{ Da}$

Because sulfoxides introduce a polar oxygen atom, oxidized impurities elute **earlier** (smaller $t_R$) than the target peptide on C18 columns.

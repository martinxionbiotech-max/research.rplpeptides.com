# Deletion Peptides Explained: Synthesis Mechanisms and Separation

!!! info "Executive Summary"
    Deletion peptides $(N-1)$ lack one or more amino acid residues due to incomplete coupling reactions during Solid-Phase Peptide Synthesis (SPPS).

## Mass Shift ($\\Delta m$)
A deletion peptide missing a Valine residue exhibits a mass shift of $\\Delta m = -99.07\\text{ Da}$. On reversed-phase HPLC, deletion peptides typically elute very close to the target peak, requiring shallow gradients for baseline resolution ($R_s \\ge 1.5$).

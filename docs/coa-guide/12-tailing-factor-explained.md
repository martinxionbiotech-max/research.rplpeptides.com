# Tailing Factor Explained: Calculation, Causes, and Mitigation

!!! info "Executive Summary"
    Peak tailing reduces chromatographic resolution and integration accuracy. Tailing factor ($T$) measures peak symmetry at $5\\%$ peak height.

## Tailing Factor Formula ($T$)

$$T = \\frac{W_{0.05}}{2f}$$

Where:
- $W_{0.05}$ is the total peak width at $5\\%$ of peak height.
- $f$ is the distance from peak apex to leading edge at $5\\%$ height.

An ideal Gaussian peak has $T = 1.0$. Acceptable SST limits specify $T \\le 1.5$.

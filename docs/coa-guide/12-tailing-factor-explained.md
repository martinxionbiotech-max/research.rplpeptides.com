# Tailing Factor Explained: Calculation, Causes, and Mitigation

Peak tailing reduces chromatographic resolution and integration accuracy.

## Tailing Factor Formula ($T$)
$$T = \frac{W_{0.05}}{2f}$$

Where $W_{0.05}$ is peak width at 5% height, and $f$ is distance from peak apex to leading edge.

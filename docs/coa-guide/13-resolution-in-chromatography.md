# Resolution in Chromatography: Formula, Peak Overlap, and Optimization

!!! info "Executive Summary"
    Chromatographic resolution ($R_s$) measures the degree of separation between two adjacent peaks. Baseline separation requires $R_s \\ge 1.5$.

## Resolution Formula

$$R_s = \\frac{2(t_{R2} - t_{R1})}{W_1 + W_2}$$

Where:
- $t_{R1}, t_{R2}$ are retention times of peaks 1 and 2.
- $W_1, W_2$ are baseline peak widths.

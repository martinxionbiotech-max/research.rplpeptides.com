# Peptide Stability Studies

!!! info "Executive Summary"
    Peptides degrade via deamidation (Asn/Gln), oxidation (Met/Trp/Cys), hydrolysis (Asp-Pro), and aggregation. pH optimization (4.0-6.0) and lyophilization maximize shelf life.

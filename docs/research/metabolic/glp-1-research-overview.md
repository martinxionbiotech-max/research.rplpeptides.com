# GLP-1 Research Overview

!!! info "Executive Summary"
    Glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) is a 30/31-amino acid incretin hormone that stimulates glucose-dependent insulin secretion, suppresses glucagon, and promotes satiety.

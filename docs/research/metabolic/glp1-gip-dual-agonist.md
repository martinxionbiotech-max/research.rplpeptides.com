# GLP-1/GIP Dual Agonists

!!! info "Executive Summary"
    Dual GLP-1/GIP agonists like tirzepatide combine complementary incretin pathways in a single molecule, producing superior glycemic control and weight reduction compared to single-receptor agonists.

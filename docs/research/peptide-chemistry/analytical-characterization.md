# Analytical Characterization of Peptides

!!! info "Executive Summary"
    Full peptide characterization requires orthogonal methods: RP-HPLC (purity), ESI-MS/MALDI-TOF (molecular identity), amino acid analysis (content), and CD/NMR (structure).

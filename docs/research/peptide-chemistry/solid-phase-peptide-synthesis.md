# Solid-Phase Peptide Synthesis (SPPS)

!!! info "Executive Summary"
    SPPS anchors the C-terminal amino acid to an insoluble resin, enabling chain elongation via repeated cycles of Fmoc deprotection, washing, and carboxyl activation.

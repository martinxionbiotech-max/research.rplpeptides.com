# Peptides in Molecular Biology Research

!!! info "Executive Summary"
    Molecular biology uses peptide epitope tags (FLAG, HA, His6), PNAs for sequence-specific nucleic acid binding, and fluorogenic peptide substrates for enzyme kinetics.

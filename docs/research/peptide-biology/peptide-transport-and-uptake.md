# Peptide Transport and Uptake

!!! info "Executive Summary"
    Small peptides cross cell membranes via proton-coupled transporters (PepT1/SLC15A1, PepT2/SLC15A2), while cell-penetrating peptides (CPPs) enter via direct translocation or macropinocytosis.

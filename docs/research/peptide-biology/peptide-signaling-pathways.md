# Peptide Signaling Pathways

!!! info "Executive Summary"
    Peptides signal primarily through G protein-coupled receptors (GPCRs) and receptor tyrosine kinases (RTKs) to activate cAMP, calcium, MAPK/ERK, and PI3K/Akt pathways.

# What Are Peptides?

!!! info "Executive Summary"
    Peptides are short chains of amino acids linked by amide (peptide) bonds, typically ranging from 2 to 50 residues in length. They function as signaling molecules, hormones, neurotransmitters, and antimicrobial agents across biological systems.

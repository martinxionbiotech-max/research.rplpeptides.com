## About This Knowledge Center

The RPL Peptides Research Knowledge Center is an independent educational platform dedicated to providing accurate, evidence-based scientific information about peptide biology, molecular research, analytical methods, and emerging peptide technologies. All content is written by the RPL Scientific Editorial Team and reviewed by the Peptide Research Advisory Team, with references sourced from peer-reviewed journals.

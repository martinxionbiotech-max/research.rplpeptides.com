## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

!!! question "What is the purpose of the RPL Peptides Research Knowledge Center?"
    The platform is an open, evidence-based educational resource dedicated to advancing scientific knowledge in peptide biology, synthesis chemistry, analytical characterization, and metabolic research.

!!! question "How are research peptide profiles formatted?"
    Each profile in our [Peptide Library](peptide-library/index.md) provides quick facts, molecular formula, sequence, target receptors, biological research background, mechanisms of action, and peer-reviewed literature citations.

<h2 style="margin-top:3rem;">Technical Review Process</h2>
<div style="margin-top:1.5rem;">
<ol style="list-style:decimal; padding-left:1.5rem; color:#475569; line-height:2;">
<li>Topic identification and scoping based on current scientific literature and research trends.</li>
<li>Drafting by subject-matter expert with comprehensive literature review.</li>
<li>Peer review by at least one additional member of the editorial team.</li>
<li>Factual verification of all citations against original peer-reviewed sources.</li>
<li>Technical accuracy review by the Peptide Research Advisory Team.</li>
<li>Style and clarity editing for consistency with platform standards.</li>
<li>Publication with DOI-style citation and permanent archive reference.</li>
<li>Quarterly review for updates reflecting scientific advances.</li>
</ol>
</div>

<h2 style="margin-top:3rem;">Content Standards</h2>
<div class="info-box info">
<p>The RPL Peptides Research Knowledge Center adheres to the following content principles:</p>
<ul style="margin-top:0.75rem; padding-left:1.25rem;">
<li>All scientific claims are supported by citations from peer-reviewed literature.</li>
<li>Content is reviewed regularly to reflect advances in scientific understanding.</li>
<li>We distinguish between established scientific knowledge and emerging research areas.</li>
<li>We do not provide medical advice, clinical recommendations, or treatment protocols.</li>
<li>References are sourced exclusively from PubMed-indexed journals and reputable scientific publishers.</li>
</ul>

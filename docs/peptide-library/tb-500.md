# TB-500 Research Profile

!!! info "Executive Summary"
    TB-500 is a synthetic peptide analog derived from thymosin beta-4 ($T\beta 4$), a 43-amino acid naturally occurring actin-sequestering peptide.

## Quick Facts
- **Functional Domain**: LKKTET actin-binding motif
- **Primary Research Areas**: Angiogenesis, cytoskeletal dynamics, cell migration, tissue repair.

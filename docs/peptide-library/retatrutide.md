# Retatrutide Research Profile

!!! info "Executive Summary"
    Retatrutide (LY3437943) is an investigational synthetic peptide engineered as a triple agonist at the GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon (GCG) receptors.

## Quick Facts
- **Peptide Class**: Multi-Receptor Agonist (Triple Agonist)
- **Molecular Targets**: GLP-1R, GIPR, GCGR
- **Half-Life**: $\approx 6-7 \text{ days}$

## Mechanism of Action
Simultaneous activation of GLP-1R (appetite suppression), GIPR (lipid buffering), and GCGR (energy expenditure & hepatic lipid oxidation) produces synergistic weight loss and metabolic improvements.

# BPC-157 Research Profile

!!! info "Executive Summary"
    BPC-157 is a synthetic pentadecapeptide (15 amino acids) derived from a stable protein fragment isolated from human gastric juice.

## Quick Facts
- **Sequence**: Gly-Glu-Pro-Pro-Pro-Gly-Lys-Pro-Ala-Asp-Asp-Ala-Gly-Leu-Val
- **Molecular Weight**: $\\approx 1419 \\text{ Da}$
- **Primary Research Focus**: Gastrointestinal protection, angiogenesis, tendon and muscle repair.

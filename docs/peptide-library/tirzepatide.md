# Tirzepatide Research Profile

!!! info "Executive Summary"
    Tirzepatide (LY3298176) is a synthetic 39-amino acid peptide engineered as a first-in-class dual agonist of the glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide (GIP) and glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptors.

## Quick Facts
- **Peptide Class**: Dual GIP/GLP-1 Receptor Agonist
- **Molecular Targets**: GIPR and GLP-1R
- **Amino Acid Length**: 39 amino acids
- **Half-Life**: $\approx 5 \text{ days}$

## Mechanism of Action
Tirzepatide simultaneously activates both GIP and GLP-1 receptors. GIPR signaling in adipose tissue and beta cells complements GLP-1R activation, producing superior glycemic control and weight reduction compared to selective GLP-1R agonists.
